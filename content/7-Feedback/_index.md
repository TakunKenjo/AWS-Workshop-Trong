> In this section, I would like to share my personal evaluations and reflections on my experience participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, as well as provide some suggestions to help improve the learning and internship experience for students in future cohorts.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working and Learning Environment**

Throughout my participation in the program, I found the working and learning environment to be comfortable, friendly, and open. Members were able to communicate, share knowledge, and support each other throughout the work process. In particular, the program provided a **WhatsApp** group that made it faster and more convenient to exchange information and ask questions. Whenever I encountered difficulties or did not fully understand an issue, I could proactively ask questions in the group and receive enthusiastic support from the Admin team.

In addition, the **AWS Study Group** community on Facebook was also a useful channel for me to stay updated with articles, knowledge, and information related to AWS, Cloud, and AI. Participating in the community gave me more opportunities to learn from practical experiences shared by others and expand my knowledge beyond the assigned project.

**2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team**

I highly appreciate the enthusiasm and support provided by the Mentors and Admin team throughout the program. Whenever I encountered difficulties during my learning process or while working on the project, I could discuss them directly and receive appropriate guidance. The team not only helped answer my questions but also provided directions that encouraged me to conduct further research and proactively solve problems on my own.

Having a common communication channel made it easier for me to ask questions whenever I encountered difficulties and saved time when looking for solutions. As a result, I became more confident when approaching new knowledge and technologies.

**3. Relevance to My Major**

The tasks and topics I was exposed to during the program were relevant to my major and career development direction. In particular, participating in project development and learning about AWS services gave me the opportunity to apply the knowledge I had learned at university in a practical environment.

In addition, I was able to gain exposure to various new topics related to Cloud, Serverless, CI/CD, AI, and AWS services. This helped me better understand how different technologies can be combined to build and deploy a practical system.

**4. Learning Opportunities and Skill Development**

The program provided me with many opportunities to improve my technical knowledge as well as essential workplace skills. Through participating in the group project, I was able to practice programming, system deployment, teamwork, and problem-solving skills while dealing with issues that arose during the development process.

In addition, writing Workshop documentation, researching technical articles, and participating in events helped me improve my self-learning, knowledge synthesis, and presentation skills. These experiences were valuable and helped me gain a clearer direction for my future professional development.

**5. Culture and Spirit of Support**

I experienced a positive spirit of cooperation and support among the members of the program. Everyone was willing to communicate when facing difficulties and share their knowledge and experience with one another. In particular, the enthusiasm of the Admin team in answering questions made me feel more comfortable when proactively discussing issues that I did not fully understand.

In addition, the **AWS Study Group** community created an open environment where everyone could share knowledge and discuss topics related to AWS. This is something I highly appreciate because it not only supported me during the internship but also provided opportunities for continued learning after the program.

**6. Policies and Benefits for Interns**

I appreciate the program's efforts to provide interns with opportunities to experience a practical learning and working environment. Participating in group projects, researching AWS services, writing Workshop documentation, and attending events gave me many valuable practical experiences.

In addition, having communication channels such as the WhatsApp group and the AWS Study Group community made it more convenient for interns to exchange information, ask questions, and access useful learning resources.

### Other Questions

**What I was most satisfied with during my internship:**

What I appreciated most was being part of a friendly learning and working environment where I could proactively ask questions and receive enthusiastic support from the Admin team. Having the opportunity to directly participate in a project and apply my knowledge in practice also helped me better understand the process of developing and deploying an application on the AWS platform.

**What I think the program could improve for future interns:**

I hope the program can organize more in-depth sharing sessions about AWS services, Cloud, and AI. In addition, more networking and interaction activities between interns and experienced members could be organized to provide more opportunities for learning and connection.

Furthermore, if regular review sessions or experience-sharing sessions could be organized at different stages of the project, interns would have more opportunities to receive feedback and guidance to improve their skills.

**Would I recommend the program to my friends?**

I would recommend the program to my friends, especially those who are interested in Cloud, AWS, AI, and want to gain exposure to a practical working environment. In my opinion, the program not only helps participants improve their technical knowledge but also provides opportunities to work on real projects, develop teamwork skills, and strengthen their ability to self-learn and solve problems.

### Suggestions and Expectations

* I hope the program will continue to maintain communication channels such as **WhatsApp**, allowing interns to conveniently ask questions and receive support when facing difficulties.
* Organize more **technical sharing** sessions or in-depth workshops on AWS, Cloud, AI, and technologies that are being used in real-world applications.
* Increase networking and interaction activities between interns, Mentors, and Admin team members to create more opportunities for learning and connection.
* Organize **regular review sessions** throughout the internship so that interns can receive feedback on their progress and work quality.
* Continue to maintain and develop the **AWS Study Group** community, creating an environment where members can share knowledge, articles, and practical experiences related to AWS and Cloud.

### Other Feedback

Overall, I feel that the **First Cloud AI Journey** program has provided me with many valuable practical experiences and knowledge. I particularly appreciate the comfortable learning environment, the enthusiastic support from the Admin team, and the convenience of communicating through the WhatsApp group. In addition, the AWS Study Group community has provided me with another valuable channel for keeping up with new knowledge and learning from the experiences shared by the community.

Through the program, I not only had the opportunity to improve my knowledge of AWS and Cloud but also developed my teamwork, self-learning, problem-solving, and practical project application skills. I would like to sincerely thank the Mentors, Admin team, and all members of the program for their support, guidance, and for creating the opportunity for me to have a valuable internship experience. I hope the program will continue to grow and provide more learning opportunities and practical experiences for students in the future.
