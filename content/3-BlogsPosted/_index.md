Here is the list of blog posts published on the [AWS Study Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/awsstudygroupfcj).

### [Blog 1 - HOW DOES AMAZON BEDROCK STOP AI-GENERATED PHISHING ATTACKS?](3.1-Blog1/)

This blog introduces how Amazon Bedrock, combined with Bedrock Guardrails and Knowledge Bases, can be used to build a system for detecting AI-generated phishing emails. The article presents a multi-layer analysis process, risk scoring, and automated email handling to enhance an organization's ability to protect against modern cyberattacks.

### [Blog 2 - AMAZON BEDROCK BASELINE ARCHITECTURE IN AN AWS LANDING ZONE: A STANDARD ARCHITECTURAL FRAMEWORK FOR SECURE GENERATIVE AI DEPLOYMENT IN ENTERPRISES](3.2-Blog2/)

This blog introduces the Amazon Bedrock Baseline architecture within an AWS Landing Zone, enabling enterprises to deploy Generative AI applications in a multi-account environment with centralized governance, security, access control, and monitoring through AWS services.

### [Blog 3 - BUILDING A SERVERLESS AI ASSISTANT AT PELAGO: FROM CONCEPT TO CARE IN JUST TWO WEEKS](3.3-Blog3/)

This blog introduces how Pelago built an event-driven Serverless AI assistant on AWS in just two weeks. The article presents how Amazon Bedrock, AWS Lambda, Amazon DynamoDB, and Amazon SNS/SQS are combined to asynchronously process patient messages, generate contextual response suggestions, and support the healthcare care team.

In particular, the solution applies a Human-in-the-Loop approach, where AI acts only as an assistant that provides suggestions and contextual analysis, while the healthcare care team reviews and adjusts the content before sending it to patients. The article also explores how Pelago leverages Serverless architecture to ensure flexible scalability, enhance healthcare data security, and optimize operational costs, while shortening the time required to bring an AI solution from concept to a production environment to just 14 days.
