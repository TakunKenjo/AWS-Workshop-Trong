### 1. SELF-ASSESSMENT

Throughout my internship at AWS from **June 22, 2026, to August 15, 2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge I acquired at university in a real-world working environment. This period helped me improve my professional knowledge, develop teamwork skills, and gradually become familiar with the process of deploying a project on the AWS cloud platform.

During my internship, I participated in the project development team and completed the assigned tasks, including:

* Developing the entire **Frontend using ReactJS**, handling APIs through **Axios**, and managing application state using **Redux Toolkit** for the SmartDocAI project.

  * Creating and using **Amazon S3** to store and deploy the Frontend.

Overall, the internship provided me with valuable knowledge and practical experience. This experience will serve as an important foundation for me to continue developing myself, enhancing my professional skills, and preparing better for my future career.
